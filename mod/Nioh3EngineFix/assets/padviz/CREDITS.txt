xbox/  (ButtonStyle = Xbox)
  "xbox_star" skin of MyGamepadViewer - https://github.com/skyjty/MyGamepadViewer (assests/svg/xbox_star, css/main.css)
  Licence: MIT, Copyright (c) 2024 skyqyzm - full text in xbox/LICENSE
  Changes: SVG layers rasterised at their CSS size (resvg), d-pad piece rotated / bumper + trigger mirrored as the CSS does,
  packed into one atlas. The Xbox controller design and the Xbox logo are Microsoft's.

Behaviour (which layer lights when, stick travel 22 px / 25 px with rotateX/rotateY tilt, trigger opacity or fill meter)
follows gamepadviewer.com's style.css / tester.js (tester.js: Copyright 2012 Google Inc., Marcin Wichary, Apache 2.0) and
MyGamepadViewer's Gamepad.js. No code from them is included.

Rebuild the atlases: py -I tools\gen_padviz_atlas.py  (Pillow + resvg-py) -> atlas_*.png, src\padviz_atlas.h, src\padviz_atlas_data.h
