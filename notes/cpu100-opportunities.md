# CPU100 pass: performance opportunities spotted while naming Unknown_ functions

Listed, not implemented. The share is perf_coverage.py --self (combined Ally+PC share of all samples) plus incl (the PC inclusive view). The "risk" is the risk of changing behaviour.

## u3 share ((rva>>4)%4==3, 98 functions), 2026-10-08

None of these is hot: every function in this share is below 0.001 % self time (max 0x866978, 0.0006 %), and the largest inclusive share is 0x2286b8 at 0.07 %. So these are fps-neutral on their own and only worth doing if a hotter caller makes one of these patterns matter.

- `0x585a74` **SharedReqSetClosedFlagAndFinalize** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.021 %): its callee 3440F80 spins on a CAS of req+28h, and when no job context is passed it backs off with Sleep(1), which can stall up to a full timer tick (about 1-15.6 ms). Fix: use a short pause-spin then SwitchToThread, or wait on an event or WaitOnAddress; risk med (threading)
- `0x4082b4` **CharacterDispatchAttachmentPoseUpdates** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.003 %): allocates and queues one 0x58-byte job (JobPushToOwner) per attachment slot, for at most 6 per character per frame, so job allocation and queue overhead likely rivals the CharacterPoseCopyAndSetup work itself. Fix: batch all of a character's slots into one job, or run them inline when the count is small; risk med
- `0x866978` **MissionEntryWithinTimeAndCountLimits** (self 0.0006 %, incl 0.000 %): each call looks up two constant params (float 0xA540, int 0x5B91) from the g_DataMgr+230h table. Its caller 866864 calls it twice per list entry every mission-thread step; fix: read both limits once per step and pass them in, or cache them at data load; risk low
- `0x2d51fc` **PhysicsStepBuildBodyBatches100** (self 0.0001 %, incl 0.000 %): every physics step heap-allocates a 60h-byte batch object for each 100 ready bodies (allocator vfunc +28h) and frees them afterwards, and walks the whole body list to filter ready bodies; fix: keep the batch objects pooled across steps and reuse them; risk low-med
- `0x3634b0` **AudioTrackedSoundsUpdate** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): for every tracked sound with an owner it computes a full SqrtfSlowPath distance just to test against 2000/300. It calls the manager getter 363AB8 several times per entry, and it holds mutex +0x2D7F0 for the whole list walk, including the command posts (6A875C/845530). Fix: compare squared distances (4e6/9e4), keep the getter result in a local, and queue the commands to post after unlocking. Risk: low (sqrt and getter changes), med (moving the posts out of the lock).
- `0x16407c` **ObjSetField114Recursive** (self 0.0001 %, incl 0.000 %): always walks the whole part tree (+0x240 children, 3 slots at +0x400), even when o+0x114 already equals v. It is called from per-frame visibility and update paths (PlaceableVisibilityUpdateRange, StageVisibilityUpdateEntries, ObjUpdateJobDispatch). Fix: return early when o+0x114 == v, which is only valid if children can never differ from their parent. Risk: med.
- `0x13087f0` **ScreenSystemSubmitLayers** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): each dead weak handle is removed from lists [10..0xB] and [0xD..0xE] with a Memcpy that shifts the rest of the list, so pruning k entries costs O(n*k), up to O(n^2). The temp draw vector also grows through VectorGrowInsert16B one element at a time with no reserve. Fix: compact each list in one erase-remove pass and reserve the temp vector to the sum of both list sizes. Risk: low.
- `0x2732b8` **CharacterTargetTrackFrameUpdate** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): per frame it calls the target getter 6181D8(c,1) about 5 times and the threshold lookup 684C04(c,7/9/0x10) several times inside the same comparisons. It also clears 0x156+0x83 bytes and copies a 0xD6-byte snapshot every tick. Fix: fetch the target and the thresholds once into locals. Risk: med (I have not confirmed that those calls have no side effects or state changes between them).
- `0x1c957c` **TextLayoutAppendGlyphOrBreak** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): for each appended glyph it can call the font-metric query FE40010 twice with the same (font, size, ch), once for ascent and again for descent when (+0xE103&0xF)==2. It also calls the 1C9E28 predicate up to 3 times before reaching the append. Fix: query the metrics once per glyph and keep the predicate result in a local. Risk: low (gain is small; it only matters for long or rebuilt text).
- `0x494ab8` **BTreeFindKeyBlobA0** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): B-tree descent runs the full key compare (u32 +10h, then memcmp of up to [key+98h] bytes) twice per entry, once for the `<0` test and again for the `==0` test; fix: compute the compare result once per entry (and binary-search the up to 18 keys in each node); risk low
- `0x138eb0` **StageVisibilityEntryRuleActive** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): each call does three successive sorted-range searches over the static rule table 46BC670..46C01F0 using (e+500h, e+508h, hash). That key never changes for a given entry; fix: cache the resolved rule pointer (or "none") on the entry and only re-run the per-call PlayerCharacterGet/ClearMapGet checks; risk low-med (unsafe if entries change stage/id without invalidating the cache)
- `0x5a6d74` **CachedFlagRefreshIfReady0C** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): resolves the same handle o+18h through 12CEEE4 twice on the refresh path; fix: resolve once and reuse the pointer; risk low (minor gain, 9 callers)
- `0x4ec3b0` **LockedCheckInitialized270** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): takes a critical section (or virtual lock) only to read one aligned 32-bit field +270h; fix: use an atomic/acquire load and drop the lock; risk med (the lock may be ordering other state that callers rely on)
- `0x3e030` **RenderLayerPruneSortSubmitEntries** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): removes dead handles from the l[10..11] and l[13..14] vectors one at a time with a Memcpy shift of the tail inside the scan loop, which is O(n^2) when many entries die together; it also builds a temporary merged 16-byte vector through VectorGrowInsert16B with no reserve on every call. Fix: compact in one remove_if-style pass, and reserve (or keep a persistent scratch vector for) the merged list sized to count(l[12..13]) + count(l[15..16]) + 1; risk low
- `0x1c85fc` **Array20BAppendGrow64** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): grows the 20-byte record array by a fixed 64 elements per realloc (12D014), so filling a large stream costs O(n^2) in copies. Fix: grow geometrically (x1.5 or x2, at least +64); risk low (watch allocator size limits)
- `0x6e77fc` **AnimClipFramesToSeconds** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): on every call it runs a hash-index probe into g_DataMgr+448h only to read the clip's frame rate (record+10h). It has 32 callers, including AnimLayerSampleClip, which calls it per layer per frame. Fix: cache the rate, or 1/rate, in the anim layer/clip instance when the clip is bound, and multiply instead of divide; risk low
- `0x5e8fbc` **AttachmentsSyncTransformAndFade** (self 0.0000 %, incl 0.000 %): for each attachment it calls the opaque getter 731A78(plVar18) about 10 times, and the compiler cannot CSE these external calls. It also makes 3 separate virtual calls on the owner (vtbl 88h/90h/98h) per entry, although the owner is the same for every entry. Fix: hoist the owner position/rotation/scale reads out of the loop, and call 731A78 once per entry and keep the result in a local; risk low
