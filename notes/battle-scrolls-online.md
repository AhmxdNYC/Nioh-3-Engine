# Nioh 3 Battle Scrolls / Eternal Rift / Shogun's Journey (web research)

Confidence: MEDIUM-LOW. Web search only; guide sites mostly gave summaries. No per-boss Battle Scroll table was found. No in-game verification.

## Battle Scrolls
- Shrine menu option. Unlocks after clearing "Crucible Manifested" (Hamamatsu Castle Town) [nerdschalk].
- Replays cleared Main Missions and Sub Missions, plus Battle-Scroll-exclusive missions [search summary of Fextralife Missions page].
- Claim "every mission is replayable" is unverified per-boss. Treat as the working hypothesis.
- Free update 2026-04-27 added more high-difficulty Battle Scroll side missions with new skill rewards [siliconera].
- Patch notes mention special Battle Scrolls (Takeda's Four Heavenly Kings) had an unlock bug, and duel-type rematches had overly fast enemy responses [dexerto].
- Shogun's Journey: Level Sync option scales you down to recommended level for better rewards. Sands of Recollection / Scarlet Sands of Despair restore combat zones [rpgsite].
- Difficulty tiers: no per-scroll tier data found beyond Level Sync.

## Eternal Rift
- Hub with training series. Warrior = Yagyu Munenori, Ninja = Hattori Hanzo, Onmyo = Saito Fuku [mapmaster].
- Tiers: Novice (immediate), Adept (after Heian), Veteran (after Bakumatsu). Veteran rewards = Smithing Texts (Great Tengu Blade, Swordmaster's Attire, Demontear Sword, Demon Fang Kusarigama, Iga Jonin Apparel).
- Boss pool is only these three NPCs (each fought repeatedly by tier) [gamingbolt].

## Shogun's Journey (NG+)
- Higher-level enemies, keep loadout. Layouts change: more enemies, humans swapped for yokai, yokai swapped for stronger variants. Prologue Edo scenes skipped. No boss-specific changes documented in sources found.

## Likely NOT refightable (gap for a rematch mod)
Unconfirmed. Candidates are anything not in a replayable mission:
- Field encounters: Demon of Pride, Demon of Envy, Demon of Obsession; field formidable enemies (Kamaitachi, Nuppeppo, Onryoki, Green Shadow's Blade, Blood-Spurting Ogin, etc.) [game8].
- Final Battle of Edo bosses (Death Veil, Hiruko, Kasha...) and prologue Tokugawa Kunimatsu, if the prologue/final are not scrollable.
- Verify in-game before building.
