# Boss per mission (online research)

Structure: 16 main missions (Game8, PCGamesN, Destructoid, VGC agree). PowerPyx and GameTrek list the in-mission objectives as separate "missions" (e.g. Escape from Hitokoto Slope, Tiger's Cause). The CSV gives the mission name with the objective in parentheses, so either name can be matched. There are 69 sub missions (GameTrek; order = its list order, not unlock order).

Sources: GameTrek per-mission pages (main source for sub bosses), Game8, PowerPyx, GamingBolt (81 bosses), PCGamesN, Destructoid, VGC. Fextralife and mapmaster were blocked or empty. nioh3.net was not used.

Open items: Shadows of the Three Oni has no named boss in any source. Rosters for Best of Five and Beneath a Pure White Flag come from GamingBolt only. The Ceremony boss (Kunimatsu duel) is GameTrek only. See the conflicts column for the rest.
