# Exit positioning and retired actor guard (#82436)

Base 5bd8a848 / BR d4d14a8f. World queue translation already existed; old replay log lines did not print actual replay coordinates. This change preserves record-to-exit offsets, captures a valid player origin even before FormHide ownership, and reanchors delayed effects at playback to the exit target's current position. Playback logs now show actual world coordinates. Ghost exit queues a fresh transition even when an earlier legacy smoke was scheduled. Recorded bone effects are remapped to the real player for exit replay, including when the old optional bone flag is off, per task.

Every form end retires its primary and recorded press actors. Capture admission, primary selection and pending-seal binding reject retired identities, and action-derived press detection ignores active/retired form actors. Retirement has no ghost timeout; removal requires observed despawn, identity/root replacement, or a fresh spawn callback for a new press. This prevents the same A2ACB tail actor from beginning another form at a changed capture timestamp.

Offline fixture covers 1000-unit extra travel, unchanged local effect offset, idempotent playback positioning, bone owner remap, primary/group retirement, delayed same-actor entry rejection and release after despawn/identity replacement. Existing hit/transition suite remains unchanged in outcome. Live visuals and input remain lead-owned; no game touched.
