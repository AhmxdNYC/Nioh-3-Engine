# Boss replay guide (player-facing)

Built from static data only (no game access), so the "How to refight" column is the best reading of the files, not something seen in play. A "?" means unsure, with the reason.

How to read the "How to refight" column:
- **Shrine -> Battle Scroll**: the mission is in the Battle Scroll list (62 entries). Rest at a Shrine, open Battle Scrolls, pick it. The scroll uses the mission's own title; where that differs from the story title I give both.
- **Mission Replay**: a story or side mission not in the Battle Scroll list; replay it from the Shrine mission menu (or NG+) and fight the boss on the way.
- **Picture Scroll**: Emaki / Picture Scroll missions.
- **NG+ only**: only by playing the mission again in a new Shogun's Journey.
- **Not refightable?**: no replay route found in the data.

"Main" = story mission, "Side" = sub mission (kitan), "Secret" = optional demon fight, "DLC" = Shadows of Edo (Keian).
