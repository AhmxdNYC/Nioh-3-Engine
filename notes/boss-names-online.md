# Nioh 3 boss list (web research, 2026-10-08)

Totals (GamingBolt/PowerPyx): about 20 main-story boss fights, 81 distinct bosses, ~109 encounters across six eras
(247 Antiquity, 1190 Heian, 1572 Warring States, 1622 Edo / Final Battle of Edo, 1864 Bakumatsu). Many bosses recur in several eras.

Main order and mission mapping come from PowerPyx (20 entries, see CSV). Sub-mission/field bosses come from the GamingBolt
81-boss table; GamingBolt gives location but not main/side labels, so those classifications are my inference.
Japanese names are only found for a few (GameWith: 塗仏, 鎌鼬, 火車, 牛頭鬼, 馬頭鬼); the rest are blank.

## Caveats
- nioh3.net's mission list conflicts with PowerPyx/GameWith (mission names look wrong/unreliable); not used.
- Fextralife and mapmaster.io were not readable (no result / 403), so cross-check is PowerPyx vs GamingBolt vs GameWith, not Fextralife.
- Main-story order is PowerPyx's; the "Jakotsu-baba" spelling (GamingBolt) vs "Yakotsu-baba" (PowerPyx) differs. Mission-to-boss pairings in rows 3-6 are single-source in part.
- No DLC info found; assume base game (released Feb 2026).

## Refight mechanics
- Battle Scrolls: unlocks after Crucible Manifested; from any shrine, pick "Battle Scrolls" to replay completed missions (nerdschalk).
- Eternal Rift: post-mission rift hub (needs Guiding Mirror from Ii Naotora) with Hattori Hanzo, Saito Fuku, Yagyu Munenori.
- NG+ "Shogun's Journey": keeps gear, resets shrines/story; switch with Heir's Journey on the map (nerdschalk).
- Three secret "Demon" ikiryo bosses are post-story (suggested level 130+).
